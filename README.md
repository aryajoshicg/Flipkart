### Flipkart — Short Summary

**Flipkart** is one of India's largest e-commerce companies. It was founded in **2007 by Sachin Bansal and Binny Bansal**, initially as an online bookstore.

Today, customers can use Flipkart to buy **electronics, mobile phones, clothing, appliances, furniture, groceries, beauty products, and many other items**.

* **Founded:** 2007
* **Headquarters:** Bengaluru, India
* **Major owner:** Walmart
* **Business model:** Online marketplace connecting customers and sellers
* **Logistics:** Ekart
* **Fashion business:** Myntra
* **Major sale:** Big Billion Days
* **Quick-commerce service:** Flipkart Minutes

In simple terms, **Flipkart is an online marketplace where customers shop for products from many different sellers, while Flipkart provides the technology, payment, logistics, and customer-service infrastructure.**
